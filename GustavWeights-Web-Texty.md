## 6 · Progress (samostatná sekce)

[Nadpis s captionem – H2]

**Caption:** PROGRESS
**H2:** See your progress, day by day

Tap Detail to see the history of an exercise on a chart. Each training day is one bar that runs from the lightest to the heaviest weight you logged that day, so you see both your top set and the range you worked in. A day with a single entry shows as a short pill.

Once you have more than five training days, swipe the chart sideways to scroll back in time.

### Every entry, editable

Below the chart is your full log, newest first, with the date and weight of every entry. Typo? Tap the entry and fix it. Logged by mistake? Swipe to delete it. You can also rename an exercise, or delete it together with all its records.

## 7 · Log it your way

[Nadpis s captionem – H2 + seznam]

**Caption:** YOUR WAY
**H2:** One app, a few ways to use it

**Top set only**
Log just your heaviest set each session. The chart becomes a clean row of your best lifts, and your PR is always a real top set.

**Every working set**
Log each set as you go. Each day's bar then shows the span from your lightest working set to your heaviest one.

**Separate rep ranges**
Create "Squat 5RM" and "Squat 1RM" as two exercises. Each gets its own PR, so a heavy single never hides your progress on fives.

**Beyond the barbell**
Weighted pull-ups, dips, kettlebells, dumbbell press: if you can put a number on it, you can track it. For weighted pull-ups and dips, log just the added weight.

## 12 · FAQ

[Nadpis s captionem – H2 + blok Podrobnosti (Details) pro každou otázku]

**Caption:** FAQ
**H2:** Good to know

**Is Gustav Weights free?**
Yes. No ads, no subscriptions, no in-app purchases.

**Does it track sets and reps?**
No, it tracks the weight. That's on purpose: logging stays fast. If you want to follow different rep ranges, create a separate exercise for each, like "Squat 5RM" and "Squat 1RM".

**Kilograms or pounds?**
Gustav Weights shows your records in kilograms.

**How many exercises can I track?**
Up to 12.

**Where are my records stored?**
Only on your iPhone. There's no account and nothing is sent anywhere, so your records don't sync between devices.

**Does it work offline?**
Yes. Gustav Weights never needs an internet connection.
